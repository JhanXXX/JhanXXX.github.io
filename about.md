Hi, I'm a PhD student at KTH in Sweden. I am affiliated with Division of Geoinformatics, School of Architecture and Built Environment. My research interest covers **geospatial AI and digital solutions for reliable cyber-physical system**. I am also interested in **transporation behaviour modeling and network analysis**. Before starting my PhD, I studied Transport and Geoinformation Technology at KTH (MSc, 2026) and Civil Engineering at [Tongji University](https://en.tongji.edu.cn/p/#/) (BEng, 2024).

**My PhD research context**: Digital Twins, GeoBIM, inverse geological modelling, sensor & telematics data analysis, logistics & optimization. **Methodologically**, this work combines geospatial data management, database technology, data science, machine learning, simulation and optimization, including model predictive control and reinforcement learning. 

Outside academia, I'm a locking dancer with experience in stage choreography and freestyle competitions. I'm also broadly interested in film & theatre arts, linguistics, video games, and music.
