# Jinghan Xu

<p class="location">Stockholm, Sweden</p>

I am a graduate student researcher in the Division of Geoinformatics and the [Integrated Transport Research Lab (ITRL)](https://www.itrl.kth.se/) at [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/en). I am due to begin my PhD in Geodesy and Geoinformatics at KTH in November 2026.

My research focuses on geospatial AI and digital solutions for reliable cyber-physical systems. I am also interested in transportation behaviour modelling and network analysis. My academic background includes MSc studies in Transport and Geoinformation Technology at KTH (expected October 2026) and a Bachelor of Engineering from [Tongji University](https://en.tongji.edu.cn/p/#/).

## Research interests

- Geospatial AI and spatial data science
- Digital twins and reliable cyber-physical systems
- Transportation behaviour modelling and network analysis
- Data-driven construction and logistics
